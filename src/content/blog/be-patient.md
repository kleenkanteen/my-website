Progress isn't happening as fast as I'd like. I'm waiting on others.

It's fine, I need to breathe and just steady my pace. Work on something else as I wait. You cannot rush people, you can only encourage.

Last week I did in-person sales and physically went to door suppliers. Made a flyer and asked to speak to someone who was in charge. Usually it worked, sometimes I got blocked.

I adapted my pitch, always be nimble in sales. I walked up confidently each time. It was work driving around but it was good progress.

Probably won't try it again given the roi and number of leads but it was a good try. 

I have to put in constant effort day in and day out. Consistent effort is the way to success. Simple statement, difficult to follow.

I'm seesawing in effort. I need to lock in.
